Honor God With a Joyful Heart

"Each of you should give what you have decided in your heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver." — 2 Corinthians 9:7

Every offering, tithe, and sacrificial gift keeps our altar sacred, sustains our priests, shelters the poor, and builds God’s house in Kabba. Your generosity touches real souls and echoes in eternity.
