Mass Intentions & Prayer Requests

Entrust your intentions to the Holy Sacrifice of the Mass or share a prayer petition with our parish community. We stand united with you in faith and prayer.

Faith & Intercession

How Can We Pray With You?

The Holy Mass is the highest form of prayer in the Catholic Church. In addition, our parish community gathers daily to hold your petitions in prayer.

Select the type of request you wish to submit below. The parish office will handle your petition with pastoral care and reverence.

Holy Mass Intentions

Have the Priest offer the Holy Sacrifice of the Mass specifically for your intention — for the repose of a deceased soul, in thanksgiving for God's blessings, for healing, or for a birthday/anniversary.

Chapel & Community Prayer Requests

Place a personal prayer petition into our Adoration Chapel Prayer Box and commend it to the maternal intercession of Our Lady Queen of Peace and our parish prayer groups.

Important Parish Notice

Mass Intention Scheduling: Submitting a request online sends your petition directly to the parish office. Mass dates are confirmed based on liturgical calendar availability. You can also make your stipend offering at the parish office or via our online giving portal.
