A Message from the Parish Priest

"Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest"

Dear brothers and sisters in Christ, whether you have been a parishioner for generations or are stepping into our church for the very first time, we welcome you with the love of Christ Jesus.

Created on 4th June 2006, Our Lady Queen of Peace Catholic Church, Odo-Afin, Kabba stands as a vibrant beacon of Catholic faith, hope, and charity. In a world full of noise and anxiety, our sanctuary offers the true peace that only Christ can bestow through the Holy Sacrifice of the Mass, sacred Scripture, and generous brotherly fellowship.

Very Rev. Fr. Michael Eniolorunda
Parish Priest

Nov 22, 2026 Solemnity

Solemnity of Christ the King & Eucharistic Procession

A joyful celebration of Christ the King, with a solemn Eucharistic procession, prayer, and Benediction. Come and proclaim Christ's kingship with the whole parish community.

Dec 08, 2026 Patronal Feast

Immaculate Conception of the Blessed Virgin Mary

A Holy Day of Obligation honouring Mary's role in God's plan of salvation. Join the parish in prayer and thanksgiving as we celebrate the Immaculate Conception with Marian hymns and devotion.

Dec 27, 2026 Feast Day

Holy Family Sunday

A joyful celebration of Jesus, Mary, and Joseph, honouring the beauty of family life and the love, faith, and unity at the heart of every Christian home.
